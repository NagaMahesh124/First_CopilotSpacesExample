# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through a lifecycle of initiation, planning, iterative execution and tracking, release, and retrospective improvement. Initiation validates the business need, goals, stakeholders, risks, and resources; planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, owners, estimates, milestones, and a Definition of Done.

During execution, teams deliver small increments and track work on a project board, with regular reviews and demos. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; the Product Manager defines outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Risks and dependencies are monitored throughout, with blockers escalated from the team through the Project Manager and Product Lead to the sponsor when needed.

Communication relies on regular team standups, weekly delivery or PM–Product Manager syncs, and monthly or milestone-based stakeholder updates, supported by a shared source of truth for status. Quality is addressed throughout development with clear acceptance criteria, code review, automated tests and linting, appropriate unit and integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, security scanning, and manual QA when needed. Releases require readiness checks, rollback planning, and post-deployment verification; retrospectives turn delivery and incident learnings into owned improvement actions.

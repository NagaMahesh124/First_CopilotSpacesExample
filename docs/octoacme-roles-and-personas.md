# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. In smaller teams, one person may cover more than one persona; teams should still make ownership and decision-making responsibilities explicit.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master or Delivery Facilitator helps the team use an effective delivery process, facilitates team ceremonies, and addresses impediments. This role supports the team and does not replace the Project Manager's accountability for overall plans, risks, and stakeholder reporting.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, daily coordination, reviews, and retrospectives as appropriate
- Help the team identify and resolve workflow impediments
- Encourage continuous improvement and make working agreements visible
- Surface delivery friction and recurring blockers

### Goals
- Help the team maintain a sustainable, predictable workflow
- Improve collaboration and reduce avoidable delays

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of team ceremonies and follow-up actions
- Regular coordination with the Project Manager on impediments and dependencies
- Working agreements and improvement actions shared with the team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with the Project Manager on schedules, dependencies, and escalations while keeping facilitation separate from project-level accountability
- Helps Developers resolve team-process impediments and improve flow
- Coordinates with the Product Manager on backlog readiness and team planning, without taking over product prioritization

## UX / Research Representative

### Role Summary
The UX / Research Representative brings user needs and usability evidence into discovery and delivery, helping the team design experiences that solve the intended problems.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or coordinate user research and usability feedback
- Communicate user needs, findings, and accessibility considerations
- Translate research insights into design guidance and actionable requirements
- Validate proposed experiences with users when appropriate

### Goals
- Ensure decisions reflect user evidence and context
- Improve usability, accessibility, and user outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Research summaries and design reviews
- Collaboration on requirements, prototypes, and acceptance criteria
- Findings shared with product, engineering, and project stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with the Product Manager to shape problem statements, validate priorities, and inform success measures
- Works with Developers to assess feasibility and clarify design intent during implementation
- Coordinates research timing, participant access, and dependencies with the Project Manager

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
The QA / Test Lead coordinates quality practices and test strategy so that the team can assess whether deliverables meet requirements and are ready for release. Quality remains a shared responsibility across the team.

### Responsibilities
- Define a risk-based test approach and identify needed test coverage
- Coordinate test planning, execution, and defect triage
- Make quality criteria and release-readiness evidence visible
- Identify recurring quality issues and recommend prevention practices

### Goals
- Detect important defects early and reduce release risk
- Provide clear, evidence-based quality information for delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, quality reports, and defect tracking
- Ongoing collaboration during development and release preparation
- Release-readiness updates with the delivery team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers on testability, automated checks, defect investigation, and fixes
- Works with the Product Manager to clarify acceptance criteria and assess product risks
- Coordinates quality milestones and release dependencies with the Project Manager

---

## Security Representative

### Role Summary
The Security Representative helps identify and address security requirements and risks throughout delivery, and brings in appropriate security expertise when needed.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on security requirements, relevant policies, and risk areas
- Coordinate threat modeling or security reviews for applicable changes
- Help track security findings and verify that mitigations are addressed
- Escalate material security concerns through the agreed governance process

### Goals
- Incorporate security considerations throughout the project lifecycle
- Make security risks, mitigations, and decisions understandable to the team

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements and review findings
- Risk and mitigation updates with project stakeholders
- Consultations during design, implementation, and release planning

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure design and implementation and reviews relevant technical mitigations
- Coordinates security review timing and dependencies with the Project Manager
- Helps the Product Manager understand security implications and trade-offs; material risk acceptance follows organizational policy and is not solely this role's decision

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder / Sponsor provides strategic direction, organizational support, and timely decisions or escalations needed to achieve project outcomes. The sponsor represents business accountability, while other stakeholders may contribute domain expertise or operational needs.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify strategic outcomes, constraints, and measures of success
- Provide or secure resources and decision-maker access
- Make timely decisions within their authority and resolve escalations
- Review progress and help align affected groups

### Goals
- Ensure the project remains aligned with organizational priorities
- Remove organizational barriers and support measurable outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and decision requests
- Project Manager status updates and escalations
- Product Manager discussions about outcomes and strategic priorities

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Aligns with the Product Manager on desired outcomes and strategic priorities, leaving day-to-day backlog ordering to the Product Manager
- Receives status, risks, and decision requests from the Project Manager and helps resolve issues beyond the team's authority
- Engages Developers or domain leads through the Product Manager or Project Manager when strategic decisions require technical or operational input

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Assign explicit ownership for each project; the personas describe responsibilities, not necessarily separate people or job titles.
